Research scientist on the UK AISI [Alignment Team](https://www.alignmentforum.org/s/NdovveRcyfxgMoujf/p/tbnw7LbNApvxNLAg8). PhD student at NYU CDS. Current research projects include: 
- Scalable oversight: the motivating question is what methods or evals would we need when training a model with debate? A high-level overview is in our debate safety case sketch [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03989)
- Aggregating natural language explanations and Bayesian methods.

I like to post about research on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/jacob_pfau) and [Lesswrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/users/jacob-pfau). In the past I've written many prediction markets e.g. ["Will an AI produce encyclopedia-worthy philosophy by 2026"](https://manifold.markets/JacobPfau/will-an-ai-produce-encyclopediawort?r=SmFjb2JQZmF1) on Manifold, and ["Will transformer derived architectures accelerate progress in deep learning?"](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/5173/transformers-effect-on-ai-research/) on Metaculus.
